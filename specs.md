# System Specifications

## Minimum Hardware Requirements
- CPU: Dual-core 2.0 GHz processor
- RAM: 4GB
- GPU: Integrated graphics (Intel HD Graphics 4000 or equivalent)
- Storage: 500MB free space
- OS: Windows 7/10, macOS 10.12+, Linux with Python 3.9+

## Performance Targets
- Framerate: 30 FPS minimum
- Memory Usage: < 2GB
- Load Time: < 5 seconds
- AI Agents: Up to 10 simultaneous agents

## AI Performance Metrics
1. Combat Effectiveness
   - Kill/Death Ratio
   - Damage dealt per round
   - Survival time
   - Win rate per round

2. Tactical Behavior
   - Team formation adherence
   - Resource collection efficiency
   - Area control percentage
   - Defensive positioning score

3. Learning & Adaptation
   - Exploration rate
   - Learning convergence time
   - Strategy diversity
   - Adaptation to enemy patterns

4. Resource Management
   - Energy efficiency
   - Ammo conservation
   - Health management
   - Power-up utilization
